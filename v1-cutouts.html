Work

  • Crews start and end shifts, fill in inspections and log their hours from a phone. Managers see vehicles, maintenance, parts and payroll hours in one place.

    • Works with no signal. Forms wait on the phone and send themselves once it reconnects.
    • Work zones drawn on an offline map of British Columbia.
    • Payroll hours with overtime rules set per person.
    • One codebase, with a separate install for each company.

    Next.js, React, Supabase, Tailwind, MapLibre

  • Budgets, accounts and savings goals that live on your phone. No sign-up, and it works offline.

    • Several accounts in different currencies, with your own exchange rates.
    • Savings goals with progress you can see.
    • Recurring payments with reminders.
    • My first project on GitHub, built with branches and pull requests.

    Expo, React Native, TypeScript, SQLite

  • The shapes up top borrow from the paper cut-outs Henri Matisse made late in his life. A random seed decides every shape, color and position, so no two visitors see the same one.

    • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step.
    • Shapes are SVG paths drawn from code, with rough edges like cut paper.
    • Drag the pieces anywhere.

    HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG
