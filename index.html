Fleet Manager

A fleet and field app for a contracting company. Crews log shifts, inspections and hours from their phones. Managers see vehicles, maintenance, parts and payroll in one place.

  • Works with no signal. Forms wait on the phone and send once it reconnects.
  • Work zones drawn on an offline map of British Columbia.
  • One codebase, a separate install for each company.
  • Next.js
  • Supabase
  • MapLibre
  • 2026

Budgeting App

A personal finance app for Android and iPhone. Budgets, accounts and savings goals that live on your phone, no sign-up needed.

  • Accounts in different currencies, with your own exchange rates.
  • Savings goals and recurring payments with reminders.
  • My first GitHub project, built with branches and pull requests.
  • Expo
  • React Native
  • SQLite
  • 2026

This site

The shapes up top are a shader that melts Matisse-style paper cut-outs together. The letters thin out and stretch as your cursor gets close.

  • WebGL fragment shader, no 3D library.
  • Variable font axes driven by cursor distance.
  • Live Discord and Spotify status.
  • WebGL
  • GSAP
  • JavaScript
  • 2026
